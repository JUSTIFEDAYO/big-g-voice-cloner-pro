📖 Comprehensive Studio & Acoustic Audio Guide
🎙️ How do I get the absolute best voice clone quality?
For true zero-shot acoustic cloning, the reference audio quality is paramount:
  • Zero Background Music or Noise: Ensure the sample has no background music, street noise, or keyboard clicks. Pure speaking voice works best.
  • Optimal Length: 7 to 15 seconds of steady, uninterrupted speech is ideal. Extremely long clips (over 45s) can dilute clarity.
  • Always Auto-Transcribe: Click "✨ Auto-Transcribe" before cloning. Supplying the reference text provides the acoustic model with ground-truth phonetic alignment.
🎚️ How should I use the Advanced Tuning Knobs?
Under "🎚️ Advanced Voice Tuning" in the Speech Studio:
  • Temperature: Keep at 0.30 - 0.50 for steady, reliable documentary narration. Raise to 0.75 - 0.90 for energetic, dramatic characters.
  • Top-P: Setting to 0.70 prevents repetitive phrasing and gives clean vowel transitions.
  • Chunk Context: Standard is 200. For complex audiobooks with long sentences, slide up to 280 for holistic prosody.
🇬🇧 How do I access UK British voices?
In the voice sidebar or voice library, click the "🇬🇧 UK" tab. You'll find Arthur (British BBC Scholar) alongside official UK neural models like Ryan and Libby.
